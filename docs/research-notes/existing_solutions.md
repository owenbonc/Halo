# Existing scam-protection products and services for UK older adults: is there a gap for "detect scam + alert trusted contact"?

All URLs accessed 2026-10-23. "Vendor" = company's own marketing or press release; "Press" = news coverage, often repeating vendor claims; "Official" = regulator, government or platform documentation; "Independent" = evaluation not run by the vendor.

## What input does each existing option see, and what intervention does it make? (comparison table)

### Takeaway
UK older adults already get layered, mostly free, mostly automatic protection on calls (network labelling, Pixel/Samsung on-device AI, iOS call screening), SMS/RCS (Google Messages, iOS unknown-sender filtering), screen-sharing (Android 16, WhatsApp) and payments (CoP, 4-day holds, reimbursement, bank call-status). Almost all of it warns the victim alone; the only UK-available mechanisms that bring in a trusted person are Monzo Trusted Contacts (Monzo-only, payment-threshold, not AI), council-issued trueCall landline blockers (trusted-number allowlists), and a small UK VoIP product (Phonely CallGuard) whose current status is unverified.

### Inferences
- The UK default stack is already strong on *volume* scams (spoofed CLI, bulk SMS, known-bad numbers) and on *after-the-fact* money (65% reimbursement rate). Remaining harm concentrates in socially engineered, coached scams where the victim acts willingly across channels (WhatsApp, new numbers, in-person couriers), which none of the built-in tools can see end-to-end.
- Nearly every built-in tool alerts only the potential victim at the moment of contact. A trusted-contact loop exists commercially only in (a) bank-specific payment friction (Monzo), (b) US-only account-monitoring services (Carefull, EverSafe), (c) call-centric family apps (Truecaller pilot, OnGuardAI US/CA, Phonely UK).
- A hackathon prototype cannot beat Google/Apple/O2 at on-device or network detection. Any defensible niche is in the *human loop*: making it effortless for an older adult to ask a trusted person "is this real?" across channels (especially WhatsApp, which Google's tools don't cover), and in coached-victim situations.

### Gaps
- Could not open Google Pixel/Messages help pages directly; UK device support for standard Messages scam detection on non-Pixel phones is contradictory across sources.
- Vodafone, Three and EE network scam-call features not verified from operator sources.
- Lloyds in-app call-status / trusted-contact equivalents not found. No other UK high-street bank "trusted contact" feature found (search turned up only a Minnesota bill).
- Samsung AI scam detection UK availability unconfirmed (only US support page found).
- Gmail/Outlook phishing filtering, Edge/Safari safe browsing, Life360, Norton Family, SilverBills, Apple Family Sharing / Recovery Contacts and Google Family Link (adult use) not researched in this pass — none surfaced as offering scam alerts to a trusted person.
- Current (2026) status of O2 Daisy and Phonely CallGuard unknown.
- "Kidas ScamShield" — no product by that name found.

## What independent evaluations or effectiveness evidence exists?

### Takeaway
Independent evidence is thin. The strongest is regulatory evaluation of payment rules (PSR) and academic work on 7726 reports and on payment-journey warnings. Almost every product-level claim (Google, O2, BT, Truecaller, Carefull, Ask Silver) is vendor-reported, measures "calls flagged" rather than losses prevented, and none is age-stratified. No Which? lab test of AI scam detectors was found.

### Cited Findings
- PSR evaluation (1 Jul 2026): APP fraud via Faster Payments fell ~21% (~£73m/yr) after mandatory reimbursement; reimbursement rate 54%→65%; outcomes still inconsistent; consultation planned Dec 2026 — [Regulation Tomorrow](https://www.regulationtomorrow.com/2026/07/payment-fraud-falls-by-73m-following-psr-reimbursement-scheme/)
- Q1 2026 dashboard: £72.6m reimbursed, ~2% of claims rejected for insufficient caution — [Plenitude](https://www.plenitudeconsulting.com/news-insights/psr-publishes-q1-2026-app-scams-reimbursement-dashboard)
- University of Nottingham CeDEx online experiment in a mobile banking app: redesigned calls-to-action "dramatically" reduced fraud success; traditional behavioural and risk-based warnings had much weaker effects — [CeDEx DP 2023-08](https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/cedex/documents/papers/cedex-discussion-paper-2023-08.pdf)
- Lending Standards Board review found scope for improving bank "effective warnings" under the CRM Code — [search summary citing Age UK response](https://ageuk.org.uk/siteassets/documents/reports-and-publications/reports-and-briefings/money-matters/consultation-response-cp22-4-authorised-push-payment-app-scams-requiring-reimbursement-the-payment-systems-regulator.pdf)
- RCT (DeLiema et al., abstract): mailed fraud-awareness intervention reduced revictimisation among known older mail-fraud victims — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11689558) (abstract only; effect sizes not verified)
- 7726 analysis (academic, NDSS 2026): 1.35M reports over 4 months at one UK operator; 89% texts; 40.27% of unique texts scams; "wrong number" most common scam type — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05276v2)
- NTS call-blocker pilot (programme self-evaluation): 108,918 calls blocked, >99% of unwanted calls blocked — [NTS](https://www.nationaltradingstandards.uk/news/successful-project-blocks-over-100000-nuisance-calls)
- Banking Protocol (UK Finance figures, 2018): 3,682 emergency calls, 197 arrests, ~£25m prevented, average £6,720 per call — [FS Matters](https://www.fsmatters.com/Banking-protocol-prevents-25m-in-fraud)
- Vendor-only metrics: Google >5M scam calls/texts flagged per month ([Google](https://blog.google/security/new-android-scam-protections/)); O2 ~70M calls/month labelled ([VMO2](https://news.virginmediao2.co.uk/ai-helps-virgin-media-o2-detect-and-flag-1-billion-suspected-scam-and-spam-calls-to-customers/)); BT 20.1M in 4 months ([Hiya](https://www.hiya.com/press-releases/bts-new-home-phone-scam-protection-service-stops-20-1-million-scam-and-spam-attempts-in-first-4-months)); Which? Scam Alerts ~£5.5m avoided ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/the-latest-scam-alerts-from-which-aBRLy2b02WkC))
- Journalist hands-on (not systematic): Norton Genie missed a vague job-offer text, otherwise accurate — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/i-tried-nortons-new-ai-scam-detector-inside-chatgpt-and-it-caught-things-i-almost-missed)

### Inferences
- "Flagged/labelled" counts can't distinguish spam from scam or show losses prevented, so there's no public evidence that any AI detector reduces older adults' losses. A hackathon project claiming better detection would have nothing to benchmark against, but also no credible way to prove superiority.
- The CeDEx finding (well-designed friction beats generic warnings) supports a human-in-the-loop or "pause and ask someone" design over another warning banner. It is not age-specific.

### Gaps
- No Which? test of AI scam detectors (Google, Apple, Norton, Scamio, Ask Silver) found.
- No independent accuracy study of Ask Silver, Scamio, McAfee, Truecaller Family, Carefull, OnGuardAI.
- No current (2024–2026) Banking Protocol figures found.
- No data on uptake of opt-in features (Pixel call detection, iOS call screening) among UK over-65s.

## Where do existing protections fail for older adults?

### Takeaway
The failures are structural: newest-device and opt-in requirements, per-app/per-bank silos, no WhatsApp content coverage, warnings that go to the victim alone (who may be coached to ignore them), and landline-only physical blockers. Reimbursement softens losses after the fact but excludes out-of-scope cases.

### Cited Findings
- **Newer devices only:** Pixel call scam detection needs Pixel 9+ (not 9a) in UK and is off by default ([search summary of Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/pixel-chat-app-scam-detection-3614116/); [Google](https://blog.google/security/new-ai-powered-scam-detection-features/)); Samsung AI scam detection needs One UI 8.5+ ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10012991/)); Messages Gemini Nano link checks need Pixel 10+/Galaxy S26 ([Google](https://blog.google/security/new-android-scam-protections/)).
- **Requires setup / opt-in:** Pixel call detection off by default ([Google](https://blog.google/security/new-ai-powered-scam-detection-features/)); Messages Enhanced protection opt-in ([Google](https://blog.google/security/new-android-scam-protections/)); iOS unknown-sender filtering opt-in ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/sg/newsroom/2025/10/apple-helps-safeguard-users-from-scams-and-fraud-while-protecting-personal-data/)); Samsung Smart Call reported off by default ([netzwelt](https://www.netzwelt.de/news/233167-versteckter-schalter-samsung-handys-ihn-umlegt-bekommt-keine-spam-anrufe-mehr.html)); Monzo controls opt-in ([Finextra](https://finextra.com/newsarticle/44398/monzo-introduces-trio-of-new-fraud-controls)); Chrome AI only under Enhanced Protection ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/google-chrome-to-use-on-device-ai-to-detect-tech-support-scams/)).
- **Over-blocking:** iOS call screening treats all unsaved numbers as suspicious, including doctors and couriers ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/06/09/ios-26-might-finally-keep-your-iphone-from-getting-spam-calls-and-texts/)); iOS 26.0 bug silencing calls despite "Never" ([Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256149537)).
- **WhatsApp:** Google's Oct 2026 chat-notification alerts don't name WhatsApp ([Google](https://blog.google/security/new-android-scam-protections/)); WhatsApp's own warnings target unknown contacts and screen-sharing ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/beware-whatsapp-screen-sharing-scams-ak3d32L0Zd5D)). Screen-sharing scams on WhatsApp are well documented ([eSecurity Planet](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/threats/whatsapp-screen-sharing-scam-drains-700k-in-minutes/)).
- **No trusted person:** None of the Google, Apple, Samsung, O2, BT protections described notify a family member ([Google](https://blog.google/security/new-android-scam-protections/) states no family features). Monzo's is the main UK bank exception and requires both parties to bank with Monzo ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/monzo-unveils-three-fraud-fighting-tools-apcKC2k65mSl)).
- **Landline-only:** trueCall works between socket/router and phone ([Carmarthenshire](https://www.carmarthenshire.gov.wales/council-services/trading-standards/nuisance-and-scam-telephone-calls/)); BT Enhanced Call Protect only for Digital Voice ([BT](https://www.bt.com/help/landline/calling-features-and-security/nuisance-calls-and-phishing/how-can-i-stop-nuisance-or-scam-calls-)); council schemes can close ([Highland](https://www.highland.gov.uk/callblockers)).
- **Reimbursement limits:** £85k cap applies to everyone including vulnerable customers; some losses fall outside scope ([Osborne Clarke](https://www.osborneclarke.com/insights/mandatory-reimbursement-uk-authorised-push-payment-fraud-countdown); [Regulation Tomorrow](https://www.regulationtomorrow.com/2026/07/payment-fraud-falls-by-73m-following-psr-reimbursement-scheme/)).
- **Older adults' perceptions:** only 8.4% of 65+ consider APP fraud their biggest fraud worry (FICO-cited survey); older respondents more likely (34% vs 25%) to feel bank warnings shift responsibility onto them (Opinium/Tunic Pay) — [Electronic Payments International](https://www.electronicpaymentsinternational.com/news/are-consumers-not-taking-app-fraud-seriously-enough/); [Financial IT](https://financialit.net/node/62468)
- **Pull-based checkers need initiative:** Ask Silver, Scamio, Norton Genie all require the user to send content in ([Metro Bank](https://metrobankonline.co.uk/ways-to-bank/i-want-some-information-about/fraud-and-security/ask-silver); [Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-gb/consumer/scamio); [SecurityBrief UK](https://securitybrief.co.uk/story/norton-unveils-genie-scam-detection-assistant-in-chatgpt)).

### Inferences
- Coached victims (bank-impersonation "safe account", police/courier, romance) are told by the scammer to ignore warnings and not tell family. On-device warnings to the victim alone are structurally weak here; a pre-agreed trusted person is the one channel the scammer doesn't control. This is the strongest argument for the concept, but I found no evidence measuring it.
- Language: Google Messages now covers Arabic, French, German, Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish besides English ([search summary of Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/pixel-scam-detection-google-messages-global-3711361)) — no South Asian languages (Urdu, Punjabi, Bengali, Gujarati) were listed, a possible gap for UK minority-ethnic older adults (inference, not verified).

### Gaps
- No UK data on what share of over-65s own Pixel 9+/Galaxy S26-class phones, or have opt-in features enabled.
- No evidence found on whether trusted-person interventions reduce losses in coached scams.

## Is "AI + caregiver/trusted-contact alert" already offered commercially?

### Takeaway
Yes. It is not novel. Truecaller Family Protection (live scam-call alerts to a family admin with remote hang-up, free, piloted outside the UK), OnGuardAI (US/Canada), Carefull and EverSafe (US financial monitoring with family alerts), Phonely CallGuard (UK VoIP, 2024, status unknown) and Monzo Trusted Contacts (UK, payment approval) all combine some form of detection with a trusted person. What I did *not* find is a UK-available, free, cross-channel (WhatsApp + SMS + calls + letters) product where the older adult and a nominated relative share an AI "is this a scam?" check, and none has independent effectiveness evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Truecaller Family Protection: launched 8 Dec 2025, free, up to 5 members with one admin; on Android admin receives alerts during suspected scam calls and can end them remotely; pilot in Sweden, Chile, Malaysia, Kenya — [Barchart (press release)](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/36503046/truecaller-launches-family-protection-to-protect-the-whole-family-from-phone-scams); [TechCrunch](https://www.techcrunch.com/2025/12/09/truecaller-now-lets-users-protect-households-from-scam-calls/)
- OnGuardAI: real-time call analysis, notifies a "Circle of Trust"; US and Canada only — [OnGuardAI](https://www.getonguard.ai/)
- Phonely CallGuard: AI on VoIP calls, alerts a "trusted person" via app, #9 brings them into the call; Jun 2024 article — [Today's Wills & Probate](https://todayswillsandprobate.co.uk/company-creates-cutting-edge-ai-product-to-protect-elderly-targets-of-phone-scammers-after-fraud-incidents-lead-to-death-and-residential-care/)
- Carefull: AI (GreyMatter) on linked financial accounts, family "circle" alerts, ScamCheck for messages; US — [Carefull](https://getcarefull.com/ai-fraud-prevention); [NBC Boston](https://nbcboston.com/investigations/consumer/new-monitoring-service-could-help-seniors-protect-themselves-from-bank-fraud/3184110)
- EverSafe: alerts to designated trusted advocates; US — [EverSafe](https://eversafe.com/apfm)
- ai'd: message/document/photo checking for older adults with one-tap trusted-contact sharing; family notifications plan "coming soon" — [ai'd](https://getaid.ai/)
- ElderShield and Scammer Guardian market SMS/email alerts to family on detected scams (vendor claims, US-marketed, unverified) — [ElderShield](https://eldershield.app/); [Scammer Guardian](https://www.scammerguardian.com/)
- Monzo Trusted Contacts (UK): nominated Monzo user approves large payments — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/monzo-unveils-three-fraud-fighting-tools-apcKC2k65mSl)
- Google: Oct 2026 Android scam announcement includes no family/trusted-contact feature — [Google](https://blog.google/security/new-android-scam-protections/)

### Inferences
- The "detect + alert family" pattern is validated commercially (multiple vendors, Truecaller at scale) but UK coverage is thin and fragmented. The gap a hackathon prototype could credibly show is narrower than "first to do this": e.g. a WhatsApp-native, consent-based "ask my person" flow where an older adult forwards anything (screenshot, letter photo, call summary) and both they and a nominated relative get a plain-English verdict, with an emphasis on coached-scam scripts ("don't tell your family", "safe account", "courier"). Even this overlaps with Ask Silver (UK, WhatsApp, free) minus the relative, and with ai'd (trusted-contact sharing).
- A prototype cannot outperform platform detection; its value would be in the escalation UX and consent model, and it should be framed as complementing built-in protections, not replacing them.
- Risk: Truecaller could switch on Family Protection in the UK at any time; Google could add family alerts. A hackathon pitch should not claim novelty.

### Gaps
- Truecaller Family Protection's 2026 rollout status (including UK) not verified.
- Phonely CallGuard's current availability, pricing and whether the company still trades not verified.
- No UK-specific consumer product combining AI scam detection + family alert across WhatsApp was found, but absence of evidence from ~40 searches is not proof of absence.
